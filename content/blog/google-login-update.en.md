For security, signing in to sitey now goes through a Google account.

## If you already had an account

**Signed up with a Google address:** go to the [sign-in page](/login) and choose Continue with Google. It is the same account, and there is nothing else to do.

**Signed up with another email:** send us the Google address you use, and we will attach your existing subdomains to it.

## Your subdomains are untouched

Only the door changed. Every record you registered is still there, and once you are in through Google you manage it exactly as before.
